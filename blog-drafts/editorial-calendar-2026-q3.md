## GEO (AI-search) checklist — applied to every post
- **Answer-first:** open each H2 with a 40–60 word direct answer to the target question.
- **FAQ schema** built from the People-Also-Ask list for that cluster (no Utah competitor is doing this — biggest GEO win).
- **Named authorities cited inline:** AUA, EAU, FDA, Cleveland Clinic, NEJM, ISSWSH/NAMS, KFF, Utah DHHS — AI engines preferentially cite content that references recognized sources.
- **Entity-rich local signals:** "Sandy," "Layton," "$650 blood-flow ultrasound (free with consult)," "Se Habla Español" — improves local AI recommendations.
- **Current data:** lead with 2025–2026 statistics; freshness drives ~76% of top AI citations.
